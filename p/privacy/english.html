Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Easy Listings.

Easy Listings ("Easy Listings," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of individuals who access or use our websites, applications, software, property listing pages, integrations, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, receive, use, store, process, disclose, and protect personal information when you interact with Easy Listings.

This policy applies to:

  • Visitors browsing our websites.
  • Registered users and account holders.
  • Real estate agents, brokers, and agencies.
  • Property owners, sellers, and landlords.
  • Prospective buyers, tenants, and other property inquiries.
  • Individuals who submit information through property listing forms.
  • Individuals who connect third-party services or integrations to our platform.

We process personal information in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection legislation, including the Constitution of the Dominican Republic, Law No. 172-13, and, where applicable, international privacy regulations.

By accessing or using our Services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Who We Are

Easy Listings is a software platform designed to help real estate professionals create, manage, publish, and distribute property listings and manage interactions with prospective clients.

Platform: Easy Listings
Operating Company: Easy Listings
Registered Address: Dominican Republic
Country of Registration: Dominican Republic
Website: https://easylistings.website
Privacy Contact: support@easylistings.website

For privacy-related questions, requests, or complaints, you may contact us using the information above.

For purposes of applicable data protection legislation, Easy Listings may act as:

  • A data controller when determining why and how personal information is processed for its own business operations.
  • A data processor or service provider when processing personal information on behalf of real estate agents, brokers, agencies, or other business customers who use our platform.

When an agent, broker, or agency collects information from prospective buyers or tenants through its property listings, that professional or business may be the controller of that information. Easy Listings generally provides the technical infrastructure to process that information on their behalf.

3. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our Services, we may collect the following categories of information.

3.1 Account and Identification Information

When you register or create an account, we may collect:

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone number.
  • Username.
  • Password credentials, stored using appropriate security measures.
  • Business or agency name.
  • Professional information.
  • Account preferences.
  • Profile photographs or logos.
  • Account role and permissions.

3.2 Property and Real Estate Information

When users create or manage property listings, we may collect:

  • Property addresses and locations.
  • Property descriptions.
  • Asking prices and rental prices.
  • Property photographs and videos.
  • Property characteristics and specifications.
  • Ownership or listing information supplied by users.
  • Availability and property status.
  • Contact details associated with listings.
  • Other information voluntarily provided by users.

Property information may be publicly displayed when a user publishes a listing.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights and permissions to publish property information and associated materials.

3.3 Prospective Client and Lead Information

When visitors submit inquiries through property listings, contact forms, or other platform features, we may collect:

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone number.
  • Country and location.
  • Preferred contact method.
  • Property preferences.
  • Budget and purchasing or rental preferences.
  • Messages and inquiries.
  • Other information voluntarily submitted through forms.

This information may be made available to the real estate professional or business responsible for the relevant property listing.

3.4 Verification and Know Your Customer Information

Certain users or real estate businesses may use features designed to collect client verification or Know Your Customer (KYC) information.

Where such features are available and activated, information may include identification details, supporting documentation, and other information voluntarily submitted for verification or legal compliance purposes.

Sensitive identification documents will only be collected where the relevant feature is enabled and a lawful purpose exists.

Users collecting this information are responsible for ensuring that they have an appropriate legal basis and provide the required privacy notices.

3.5 Payment and Subscription Information

When you purchase a subscription or paid service, we may collect:

  • Subscription plan.
  • Billing details.
  • Transaction identifiers.
  • Payment status.
  • Invoice information.
  • Billing history.

Payments may be processed by independent third-party payment providers.

Easy Listings does not intend to store complete payment card numbers or security codes on its own servers when payment processing is handled by an external provider.

Payment providers process payment information under their own privacy policies and applicable obligations.

3.6 Technical and Device Information

When you access our Services, we may automatically collect:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type and operating system.
  • Language preferences.
  • Approximate geographic location derived from technical information.
  • Referring website.
  • Pages visited.
  • Access timestamps.
  • Session information.
  • Diagnostic and error information.
  • Cookies and similar technologies.

3.7 Third-Party Authentication Information

If you choose to connect or authenticate using third-party services, such as Google, Meta, or Pinterest, we may receive information that you authorize those services to share with us.

Depending on the integration, this may include:

  • Account identifiers.
  • Name.
  • Email address.
  • Profile information.
  • Authentication tokens.
  • Authorized account or business information.
  • Information necessary to perform the integration you requested.

We only access information permitted by your authorization, the applicable provider's API permissions, and its developer policies.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

  • Client inquiries: For as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant real estate transaction, customer relationship, or applicable retention requirements.
  • Payment records: As required by applicable accounting, tax, and financial regulations.
  • Technical logs: For security, troubleshooting, and operational purposes.

When personal information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it.
